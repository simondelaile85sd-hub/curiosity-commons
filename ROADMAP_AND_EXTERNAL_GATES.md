## Protocol-freeze decision

Only after the gates are resolved should the project decide whether to:

- freeze a host-specific Pilot A protocol;
- redesign and repeat external review;
- partner with an existing institution instead of creating a separate pilot;
- reduce the test further; or
- stop.

Recruitment, scheduling, public promises, fundraising tied to outcomes, and participant data collection occur only after a separate go/no-go decision.

## Pilot A learning sequence

If authorised:

1. run the smallest viable manual setting;
2. monitor safety, pressure, access, hidden labour, hierarchy, facilitator dependence, and data continuously;
3. pause or stop before protecting activity metrics;
4. report null, negative, adverse, contradictory, and comparative findings;
5. decide whether any mechanism is worth retaining; and
6. do not add AI, credits, commerce, or federation merely because the pilot ran.

## Parked later work

The following are not Pilot A deliverables:

- participant-facing AI or algorithmic matching;
- time credits, mutual credit, or another exchange ledger;
- paid participant marketplace or enterprise layer;
- capacity-relative contribution accounting;
- Story Commons or comparative story ecology;
- cross-site federation and replication;
- shared ownership, asset-lock, trust, cooperative, or mutual structure;
- social-security or benefits integration;
- longitudinal social-network analysis; and
- claims of scale or cost-effectiveness.

Each requires its own rights, evidence, governance, and failure analysis. Some may never proceed.

## Public update rule

A roadmap item moves to “complete” only with a dated, reviewable output. Percent-complete estimates and historical maturity scores are not evidence and are not used in this public release.
