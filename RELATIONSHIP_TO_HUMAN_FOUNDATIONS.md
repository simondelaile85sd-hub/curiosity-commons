# Relationship to Human Foundations for an Age of AI

**Status:** Current legal, conceptual, and evidential boundary statement.

## 1. Relationship

[Human Foundations for an Age of AI](https://github.com/simondelaile85sd-hub/human-foundations-age-of-ai) is a broader public working framework concerned with questions of agency, belonging, resilience, value, and institutional design in an age shaped by AI.

Curiosity Commons is a separate project that may operationalise and stress-test a subset of those questions through a specific social-infrastructure architecture and a proposed manual-first pilot.

The relationship is real, but it is not a validation chain.

## 2. Two-way non-transfer rule

1. Human Foundations does not validate Curiosity Commons.
2. Curiosity Commons does not validate Human Foundations as a whole.
3. Evidence transfers only to the precise mechanism or proposition actually tested.
4. A shared author, development history, vocabulary, or link does not merge the projects legally or evidentially.
5. Each repository has its own licence, scope, rights statement, version history, and claim register.

## 3. Examples

| Possible finding | Permitted inference | Prohibited inference |
|---|---|---|
| Some Pilot A participants report welcome without contributing | The particular belonging-first practices may have been workable in that site and period | Human Foundations' account of belonging is validated generally |
| The discovery board creates pressure | That board design should be stopped or redesigned; the mechanism may contradict the project commitment | Belonging-first institutions are impossible, or Human Foundations is false as a whole |
| One neutral invitation lowers a reported access barrier | The opt-in invitation may have helped that specific mechanism/context | Bidirectional belonging is established as a general law |
| Facilitator burden is unsustainable | The protocol or host model may not be viable | Human facilitation never works, or AI matching is therefore justified |
| An existing neighbourhood house performs the function better | A separate Curiosity Commons institution may be unnecessary in that context | The broader Human Foundations questions have been answered |

## 4. Legal and rights separation

The Human Foundations licence covers only the original material identified in that repository. It does not license the private Curiosity Commons archive or automatically determine this repository's licence.

Likewise, this repository's eventual licence will cover only material expressly included here and within the boundary in [RIGHTS_AND_PROVENANCE.md](RIGHTS_AND_PROVENANCE.md). It will not relicense Human Foundations, external sources, private conversations, unreleased archives, participant material, or third-party rights.

## 5. Citation practice

When a Curiosity Commons document draws on a Human Foundations proposition, cite the exact proposition or section rather than the project name alone. If Pilot A later produces evidence, report which hypothesis, mechanism, population, site, period, instrument, and comparison the evidence concerns.

Avoid phrases such as “based on a validated framework,” “proof of the Human Foundations thesis,” or “Human Foundations implementation” unless a future release defines and supports that exact claim.
