# Curiosity Commons — Pilot Interpretation and Cognitive Access Addendum v0.2

**Date:** 14 October 2026  
**Status:** Provisional design and interpretation questions; not a validated instrument or approved protocol. Supplements [Pilot A protocol](PILOT_A_PROTOCOL.md), [measurement instruments](MEASUREMENT_INSTRUMENTS.md), and [safeguards](SAFEGUARDS_PAUSE_AND_FAILURE_THRESHOLDS.md). No recruitment or operation is authorised by this addendum.

## Cognitive cost of voluntary participation

Pilot A already permits quiet presence, refusal, withdrawal, and low-pressure participation. Review whether exercising these rights imposes hidden cognitive or social costs: navigating invitations, interpreting expectations, explaining refusal, seeking accommodations, or returning after an absence.

**Review question:** Can someone attend, pause, decline, leave, or return without justification, repeated disclosure, or a perceived penalty? Does the setting accommodate differing levels of attention, energy, and communication capacity without assigning diagnoses or fixed identities?

## Non-participation is not a diagnosis

A missed session, unused discovery board, declined introduction, incomplete form, or silence is an observation, not evidence of disinterest, exclusion, satisfaction, incapacity, or failure. Possible explanations include preference, competing obligations, fatigue, safety, accessibility barriers, pressure, or a satisfactory alternative elsewhere.

**Interpretation rule:** Separate observation from competing interpretations, hypotheses, tests, and revision. Preserve uncertainty. Do not infer motives, vulnerability, or participant traits or create person-level dossiers. Seek clarification only through ethically reviewed, genuinely optional methods.

## Optionality versus implicit obligation

Examine whether repeated invitations, attention concentrated on visible contributors, praise, or facilitator expectations turn nominally voluntary activity into felt obligation. Refusal and non-return are not retention problems to optimise away.

**Potential adverse signal:** Participants feel obliged to perform sociability, contribute, or explain absence to remain welcome.

## Measurement and change control

Existing instruments already address refusal, quiet participation, access, pressure, and non-return. This addendum does **not** silently alter their questions. Before any pilot, reviewers may consider whether a short optional prompt about navigation burden or ease of disengagement adds sufficient value to justify its cost and privacy implications. Any revision needs methods, accessibility, consent, and version review; incompatible results must not be combined without disclosure.

## Pilot boundaries remain unchanged

Pilot A remains manual-first and excludes participant-facing AI, automated matching, scoring, commerce, and other deliberately deferred components. This addendum proposes interpretation and accessibility refinements, not a broader pilot. The existing external legal, safeguarding, host, and operational gates remain in force.

These are unvalidated project-synthesis questions, not empirical findings. [Human Foundations](https://github.com/simondelaile85sd-hub/human-foundations-age-of-ai) remains a separate project; neither validates the other.
