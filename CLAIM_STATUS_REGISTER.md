# Claim-status register

**Status:** Current publication-control register, 15 September 2026.

## 1. Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning in this repository |
|---|---|
| Normative commitment | A project choice about what should be protected; not an empirical finding |
| Established prior art | A documented concept, institution, practice, standard, law, or evidence base that predates the project |
| Project synthesis | A Curiosity Commons arrangement of ideas; not a novelty or efficacy claim |
| Hypothesis | A proposition that may be tested or challenged |
| Operational proposal | A concrete way to run or measure something; unvalidated until reviewed and tested |
| External gate | A required determination outside the documents, such as host, legal, accessibility, safety, or lived-experience review |
| Superseded | Replaced for the stated purpose by a later version; retained privately for history |
| Parked | Deliberately outside the current pilot or release scope |
| Unknown | Evidence or decision is presently insufficient |

“Canonical” in this repository means current for project version control. It does not mean true, validated, legally effective, or permanently fixed.

## 4. Change rule

A claim may move status only when the release record identifies:

- the exact claim;
- the new evidence or decision;
- who reviewed it;
- the scope and limitations of transfer;
- the version changed; and
- any contradictory evidence retained.

Public wording should become no stronger than the evidence permits.
