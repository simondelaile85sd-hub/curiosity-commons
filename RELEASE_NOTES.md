# Release notes

## Public working release v0.1 — candidate prepared 15 September 2026

### Purpose

This release reconstructs Curiosity Commons from its dedicated project files and later corrections rather than from a summary or the Human Foundations README. It publishes a privacy-clean, rights-bounded synthesis instead of the private archive.

### Current material represented

- capacity-discovery architecture;
- staged belonging-to-optional-earning pathway;
- plural exchange and economic-membrane design;
- reciprocity, governance, anti-capture, and hostile-adoption work;
- hidden-labour, contributor-hierarchy, facilitator-power, and accessibility safeguards;
- Pilot A Protocol v0.2;
- Pilot A safeguards, pause, and failure thresholds v0.1;
- Pilot A measurement instruments v0.1;
- evidence, prior-art, provenance, and lens crosswalk;
- capacity-relative contribution, solidarity-buffer, and capacity-ceiling proposals, marked unvalidated;
- generative, replication, continuity, social-agency, interaction-acquisition-cost, and bidirectional-belonging extensions, marked developing or parked;
- the two-way evidence boundary with Human Foundations; and
- the external/host gate reached after the 17 August 2026 internal progression pass.

### Material superseded for Pilot A

The 9 August 2026 minimum viable pilot canvas was an earlier, broader design. Pilot A Protocol v0.2 supersedes it for first-pilot scope.

The current Pilot A deliberately excludes:

- the earlier participant-facing AI station;
- algorithmic matching;
- points, credits, or currency;
- commerce;
- federation;
- social-security mechanisms; and
- professional-service substitution.

The broader questions are not treated as absent; they are outside the first causal test.

### Corrections preserved

- Later protocol, safeguard, measurement, and crosswalk files supersede older statements that those artefacts did not exist.
- Dedicated project files govern over summaries and historical container exports.
- Historical percentage-complete estimates are planning heuristics, not evidence.
- Human Foundations and Curiosity Commons are legally and evidentially separate.
- AI assistance is identified as operationalisation, synthesis, stress-testing, source discovery, and packaging—not validation or origin of Simon Delaile's normative project direction.
- The release makes no component or overall novelty claim.

### Privacy and rights exclusions

No private conversation, private review exchange, personal anecdote, third-party identity, participant material, private website, unpublished archive, culturally restricted material, Story Commons content, or unclear-rights source file is published.

External works are cited, not copied or relicensed. The release contains original prototype questions rather than reproducing an established questionnaire.

### Validation status

There has been no live Curiosity Commons pilot and no external validation. Pilot A remains blocked on host, legal, insurance, WHS, volunteer/employment, safeguarding, accessibility, privacy, methods, participant-legibility, and facilitator-resource gates.

### Known limitations

- The prior-art review is targeted, not systematic.
- The architecture may duplicate existing community institutions.
- The measurement instruments are prototypes.
- No legal, financial, ownership, or governance entity has been selected.
- No recruitment or participant-facing material is authorised.
- The public synthesis cannot replace lived-experience or culturally authorised review.

### Release method

The public files are curated derivatives with explicit status labels. Simon Delaile selected CC BY-SA 4.0 for the bounded public release on 15 September 2026. Private source files remain private for provenance, correction, and supersession history. The canonical public record will be the tagged Git commit identified in the publication record after approval.
