## 2. Human-originating project direction

The development record attributes the following normative and project ideas to Simon Delaile, with some phrasing refined through dialogue:

- belonging before productivity;
- capacities discoverable without becoming obligations;
- voluntary contribution and refusal without penalty;
- the pathway from belonging and encounter through discovery, learning, and practice toward optional helping, teaching, or earning;
- plural exchange rather than one totalising ledger;
- equal human standing despite unequal achievement;
- solidarity, sustainable capacity, maintenance, and hidden-labour concerns;
- an economic membrane between belonging and formal work or commerce;
- anti-capture and anti-domination as design requirements;
- the distinction between AI revealing or assisting possibilities and humans making relationship real; and
- interaction acquisition cost and bidirectional belonging as social-agency questions.

This is a provenance statement, not a novelty claim. External traditions and institutions cover many related components.
